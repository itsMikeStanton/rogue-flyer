Keyboard controls
  • W / S — pitch (nose down / up)
  • A / D — roll left / right
  • Q / E — yaw (rudder)
  • Shift / Ctrl — throttle up / down  ·  hold Shift at full throttle — afterburner (fighters; no guns while lit)
  • Space — cannon  ·  B — missile  ·  R — rockets  ·  N — bomb  ·  X — flares
  • G — gear  ·  V — flaps  ·  Z — airbrake  ·  T — VTOL nozzles (Harrier)  ·  H — vehicle bay
  • C — camera (Chase / Far / Cockpit / Rear)  ·  Y — flyby  ·  U — bomb sight (bombers)
  • K — toggle radar / target markers  ·  J — toggle HUD  ·  L — landing-approach guidance  ·  O — tactical map  ·  P — flight-plan route on/off  ·  F — fullscreen  ·  M — mute  ·  Esc — pause menu

Action mapping — keyboard & joystick

Each action can live on a keyboard key and/or a joystick button — click a slot, then press the key/button. ✕ turns it off to free that input. Binding an input already in use asks before stealing it. (By default flight & weapons sit on the stick; camera & system controls on the keyboard.)
