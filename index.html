Keyboard controls
  • W / S — pitch (nose down / up)
  • A / D — roll left / right
  • Q / E — yaw (rudder)
  • Shift / Ctrl — throttle up / down
  • Space — cannon  ·  B — missile  ·  R — rockets  ·  N — bomb  ·  X — flares
  • G — gear  ·  V — flaps  ·  Z — airbrake  ·  T — VTOL nozzles (Harrier)  ·  H — vehicle bay
  • C — camera (Chase / Far / Cockpit / Rear)  ·  Y — flyby  ·  U — bomb sight (bombers)
  • K — toggle radar / target markers  ·  J — toggle HUD  ·  L — landing-approach guidance  ·  F — fullscreen  ·  M — mute  ·  Esc — pause menu
